Silicon Valley teaches scale. Japanese manufacturing teaches quality
Which matters more when you need 2,000 new businesses?

Japan has a national goal: to capture one-third of the global AI robotics market by 2040.
 But there is a question that bothers me.

If the market is really a long-tail market, success may not come from a few giant companies alone.
It may require thousands of specialized businesses serving different industries and different customers.

Japan has more than 6,000 university startups today.
Yet only a small number have reached meaningful commercial scale.
This is not a criticism.
It is a question.

Do we need a new generation of people who can connect technology, quality, and markets?

Perhaps the AI robotics era will need both.
